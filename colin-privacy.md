# Privacy Policy for Colin - My Phone Hates Me
Last updated: 29 September 2026

**Overview:** Colin is an offline screen-time helper. No login, no account.

**Data Collection:** We do NOT collect, store, or share any personal data. No name, email, location, contacts, photos. No analytics. No ads.

**Parental Function:** Any time limit set by a parent is stored locally on that device only. It is never sent to us or any server.

**Permissions:** 
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS (Usage Access) - Used only on-device to measure screen time so Colin can change mood from green > grey > red. This data never leaves your device.

**Children:** Colin does not collect personal data from children under 13. All processing is on-device.

**Third Parties:** None.

**Contact:** marlsoftappdev@gamil.com

**GDPR:** As no personal data is collected, there is nothing to store or delete. Contact us if you have questions.
