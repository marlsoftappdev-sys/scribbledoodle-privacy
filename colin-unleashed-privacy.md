# Privacy Policy for Colin Unleashed - My Phone Hates Me

Last updated: 29 September 2026

**Overview:** Colin Unleashed is an 18+ horror parody version of Colin. Contains disturbing imagery, dark humour, and jump-scare style audio. This version is for adults only (18+). No login, no account, offline play.

**Age Rating:** This app is rated 18+ / Mature. It is not intended for children. You must be 18 or older to install.

**Data Collection:** We do NOT collect, store, or share any personal data. No name, email, location, contacts, photos. No analytics. No ads. No tracking.

**How it Works:** All gameplay, including screen-time reactions and horror events, is processed locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.

**Permissions:**
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS (Usage Access) - Optional, used only on-device to measure screen time so Colin's behaviour can change. This data never leaves your device. You can deny this permission and the game still works.

**Third Parties:** None. No AdMob, no Firebase, no analytics SDKs.

**Children:** This 18+ version does not target children and does not collect data from children.

**Contact:** marlsoftappdev@gmail.com

**GDPR / UK:** As no personal data is collected, there is nothing to store or delete. Contact us with any questions.
