## The short version

The App collects **no personal data**, requires **no account**, contains **no
ads**, and works **entirely offline**. We have no servers and receive nothing
from your device.

## What the App stores (on your device only)

Your progress — stars per level, coins, skip count, and settings such as sound
and haptics preferences — is saved **locally on your device** using Android's
standard local storage (SharedPreferences). This data:

- never leaves your device,
- is not readable by us (we have no access mechanism),
- is deleted if you uninstall the App.

## Permissions

The App requests a single permission: **Vibrate** (so button presses can give
haptic feedback). It does not request internet, location, contacts, storage,
camera, microphone, or any other permission.

## Children

The App is a word-puzzle game appropriate for all ages. It contains no chat,
no user-generated content sharing, no advertising, and no data collection of
any kind, so no personal data of any user — child or adult — is processed.

## Third-party services

None. The App embeds no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social
SDKs. All fonts and artwork are bundled in the App itself.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App ever adds networking, advertising, or data
collection, this policy will be updated **before** that version is released,
and the Play Store Data Safety form will be updated to match.
